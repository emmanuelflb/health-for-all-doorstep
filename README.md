# Market Doctors For All

Design an ngo website with the name - MarketDoctors The website introduces visitors on what we do. some content: Market Doctors is a social impact enterprise that provides affordable and accessible basic healthcare to Nigerians, especially those in the informal sector, rural areas and slums, who either do not have access to quality healthcare or cannot afford it. Our purpose is to serve people in need of care, with professionalism, igniting hope, and taking health care to the door step of Nigerians without disturbing their day to day activities. We believe in a world where people can have access to basic health services irrespective of their location, religion, ethnic group and social class

OUR VISION: Bringing affordable healthcare to the doorstep of Nigerians with minimal disturbance to their economic activities and incorporating healthcare delivery into their daily activities. OUR MISSION: Towards an accessible, sustainable and affordable, preventive and curative primary healthcare for the informal sector, underserved and hard to reach areas. OUR CORE VALUES: Professionalism, Impact Driven, Commitment, Collaboration. OUR SERVICES: Medical outreaches, Management of Mobile Clinics, Retails Clinics, Health Education & Sensitization, Health-Related CSR, Training and empowerment of Youths and Women as Health connect, Procurement of health supplies.

Weoffer accessible, affordable medical assistance to local residents, ensuring timely treatment for common illnesses and health concerns. Providing vaccinations, health advice, and referrals to specialists if needed.

FOCUS AILMENTS: Non-Communicable Diseases (accounting for more than 60% deaths including hypertension and diabetes screening), Malaria (a known cause of recycling poverty in Nigeria), Eye Care (basic eye screening for reading, to improve productivity and prevent blindness), Maternal health

SOME OF OUR PARTNERS: MTN Foundation, Exxon Mobil, Shalina Healthcare, Emzor Pharmaceutical, Vision Spring, Nigerian Breweries, LAWMA, Total Nigeria, BetKing, PeacanTrust Microfinance Bank, His Love Foundation, SwiPha
